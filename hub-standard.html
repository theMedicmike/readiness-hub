The Standard

The promise

Honest by design

The standard is free to read and openly versioned. Its guiding rule is simple:

The Honesty Compact: "We will always tell you the truth about what we know, what we think, what is still being studied, and what nobody yet knows. We will never oversell a benefit or hide a risk. This supports an informed decision with a medical team — it does not replace clinical care or consent."
What it covers

One Common Core + a safety annex per medicine

The universal spine applies to every treatment; each medicine adds its own specific dangers and screening.

1 · Screen

Universal medical + cardiovascular + psychiatric screening and full medication reconciliation.

2 · Prepare

Conservative, test-first terrain optimization and psychological + caregiver preparation.

3 · Clear-to-Treat

A documentation handoff — the go/no-go always belongs to the treating clinician.

Modality annexes: Ibogaine (cardiac) · MDMA (serotonin/heat) · Ketamine (bladder/dependence) · Psilocybin & LSD (psychological/HPPD) · Cannabis (psychosis) · Kratom (liver/dependence) — extensible to any new medicine.

Integrity

Governance & how it stays honest

  • Independent standards council — a psychedelic-medicine physician, toxicologist, neuropharmacologist, ethics member, plus veteran & caregiver representatives.
  • Named Medical Director owns all clinical content, thresholds, and sign-off — version-controlled with a public change log.
  • Conflict-of-interest firewall — the standards function is separate from any product sales; nothing here is ever contingent on buying anything.
  • Public & free — the standard is open to read and free to use. We certify no one: no program, provider or caregiver.
